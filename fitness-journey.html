Week 1

Gentle workout guide

Workout A: Chair sit-to-stands, wall push-ups, supported heel raises, seated marching.

Workout B: Supported side steps, comfortable standing hip movements, shoulder-blade squeezes, seated knee extensions.

Begin with one comfortable set of 4–8 repetitions per movement, rest freely and stop anything that worsens pain. For your sister’s persistent back pain, a physiotherapist or GP should advise on appropriate movements before progressing.
